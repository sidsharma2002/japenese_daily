One Japanese work greeting is safe for your boss, the other can sound rude. お疲れ様 vs ご苦労様.

お疲れ様です (otsukaresama desu) = thanks for your hard work. Fine with coworkers and your boss.
ご苦労様 (gokurousama) = good work, said from above. A boss can say it to the team, but you should not say it upward.

If your boss says ご苦労様 to you, answer with お疲れ様です.
Not sure who is listening? Pick お疲れ様です.

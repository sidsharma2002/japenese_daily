Three myths about Japanese words for plans, promises and habits. Busted.

Myth 1: 予約 (よやく) is a promise. No, it is a reservation. A promise to a person is 約束 (やくそく).

Myth 2: 計画 (けいかく) and 予定 (よてい) are the same. 予定 is what is already on your schedule. 計画 is a plan you build.

Myth 3: 続く (つづく) and 続ける (つづける) are the same. 続く happens by itself, like rain. 続ける is you keeping it going, like studying every day.

A booking is not a promise, and a schedule is not a plan.

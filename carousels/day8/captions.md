Someone offers you tea and you say いいです. Your host may not know if that is yes or no thanks.

いいです (ii desu) after an offer can mean good, or I am fine without it.

For yes: はい、お願いします (hai, onegai shimasu) = yes, please. いただきます (itadakimasu) = I will gladly have it.
For no: ありがとうございます。遠慮させていただきます (enryo sasete itadakimasu) = thank you, I will politely pass.

Exception: if someone asks いいですか (is it okay?), はい、いいですよ is a clear yes.

Save it now, use it this week.

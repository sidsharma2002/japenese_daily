## Instagram (slide-1.png ... slide-N.png)

These 5 kanji run your whole week: 予 約 決 続 慣

予 (ヨ) beforehand: 予定 yotei, plan
約 (ヤク) promise: 約束 yakusoku, a promise or appointment
決 (ケツ / き-める) decide: 決める kimeru, to decide
続 (ゾク / つづ-く) continue: 続く tsuzuku, to continue
慣 (カン / な-れる) get used to: 習慣 shuukan, habit

Plan, promise, decide, continue, habit. One chain you can read now.

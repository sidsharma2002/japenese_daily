<excited> Three myths about Japanese words for plans, promises and habits. Let's bust them.

<confident> Myth one. よやく means promise. Not true. It means a reservation, like booking a table. A promise to a person is やくそく.

<confident> Myth two. けいかく and よてい are the same. Not quite. よてい is what is already on your schedule, like, I have plans tomorrow. けいかく is a plan you build, like a trip plan.

<confident> Myth three. つづく and つづける are the same. Not quite. つづく is something that goes on by itself, like rain. つづける is you keeping it going, like, I keep studying every day.

<excited> Remember, a booking is not a promise, and a schedule is not a plan.

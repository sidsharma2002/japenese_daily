Three myths about Japanese words for plans, promises and habits.

Myth 1: 予約 (よやく) means promise. No, it is a reservation. A promise to a person is 約束 (やくそく).
Myth 2: 予定 (よてい) and 計画 (けいかく) are the same. 予定 is what is already on your schedule. 計画 is a plan you build.
Myth 3: 続く (つづく) and 続ける (つづける) are the same. 続く is something that goes on by itself. 続ける is you keeping it going.

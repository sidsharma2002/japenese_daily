A friend cannot come to your party. Here is the word to say back, and why it works.

残念です (ざんねんです) = that is too bad.
残 (のこる) = remain
念 = thought
A thought that stays with you.

残る (のこる) = to remain: ケーキが一つ残っています。One cake remains.
念のため (ねんのため) = just in case: 念のため、メモします。Just in case, I will write it down.

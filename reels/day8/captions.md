## Instagram (reel.mp4, cover.png)

This looks like the English word claim, but in Japan it means something else: a complaint.

クレーム (くれーむ) = a complaint.
お客様からクレームが来ました。 = We got a complaint from a customer.
クレームをつける = to lodge a complaint.
苦情 (くじょう) = a more formal word for complaint.

The word comes from English claim, but Japanese kept the sound and the meaning moved.

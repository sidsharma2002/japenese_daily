Japanese has one word for people who quit after three days: 三日坊主 (mikkabouzu), the three-day monk.

Why it happens: big plans need big days, and one missed hour makes the whole plan feel broken.

The fix: set a floor, not a goal. Mini mode is fifteen minutes: five words, one grammar point, five minutes of listening. It still counts. And miss once, never twice.

I built a 45-day Japanese habit plan on this rule: one page a day, N4 to N3 to an N2 starter.

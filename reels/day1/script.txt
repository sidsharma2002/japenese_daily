Read as a warm, clear Japanese teacher talking to one learner: relaxed, friendly, steady pace, slow and clear on the Japanese words.

<excited> Never use ことにする for a daily habit. It makes your habit sound like a one-time decision.

<confident> Rule one. Use ようにする for effort. It means, I make a point of doing it. Like, I make a point of listening to Japanese every day. You keep trying, again and again.

<confident> Rule two. Use ことにする for a decision. It means, I decided to. Like, from today, I decided to study thirty minutes every morning. You chose it once, and that is it.

<confident> Rule three. Run a quick test before you speak. Ask yourself, did I decide, or am I trying? If you decided, say ことにする. If you are trying, say ようにする.

<excited> Remember, こと is a decision. よう is an effort.

<upbeat> Follow for one Japanese tip every day.

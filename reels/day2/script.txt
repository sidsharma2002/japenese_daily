<excited> Do you know why one work greeting is safe for your boss, and the other can sound rude?

<confident> First, おつかれさまです. It means, thanks for your hard work. Say it to coworkers, and to your boss. It is the safe choice at work.

<confident> Second, ごくろうさま. It also praises someone's effort. But it sounds like a boss talking down to the team. Use it only toward people below you, if at all.

<confident> Here is the test. Ask yourself, who is listening? Your boss, or you are not sure? Pick the safe one. Only someone below you? The other is possible, but never upward.

<excited> Remember, never say the second one to your boss.

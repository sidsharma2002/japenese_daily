Do you know why one Japanese work greeting is safe for your boss, and the other can sound rude?

お疲れ様です (otsukaresama desu) = thanks for your hard work. Safe with coworkers and your boss.
ご苦労様 (gokurousama) = good work, said from above. It can sound like talking down, so keep it for people below you, if at all.

Not sure who is listening? Pick お疲れ様です.

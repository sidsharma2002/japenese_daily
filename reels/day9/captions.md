Three lines will get you through any konbini checkout.

1. 温めますか (atatamemasu ka) = shall I heat it up? Answer: お願いします (onegai shimasu) = yes, please.
2. 袋は要りますか (fukuro wa irimasu ka) = do you want a bag? Answer: 大丈夫です (daijoubu desu) = no thanks, I am fine.
3. お箸をお付けしますか (ohashi o otsuke shimasu ka) = shall I add chopsticks? Answer: いらないです (iranai desu) = I do not need them.

If the clerk forgets to ask: 温めてください (atatamete kudasai) = please heat this.

Save this for your next trip to Japan.

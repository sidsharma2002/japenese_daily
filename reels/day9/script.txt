Relaxed and vivid, like describing a scene to a friend. Slow and clear on the Japanese.

<excited> Three lines will get you through any konbini checkout, even on your first day in Japan.

<confident> First, you are holding a bento at the counter. The clerk asks, あたためますか. That is, shall I heat it up? Say, おねがいします. You are saying, yes, please.

<confident> Second, the bag. The clerk asks, ふくろは、いりますか. That is, do you want a bag? To say no thanks, say, だいじょうぶです. You are saying, I am fine.

<confident> Third, the chopsticks. The clerk asks, おはしを、おつけしますか. That is, shall I add chopsticks? To pass, say, いらないです. You are saying, I do not need them.

<excited> Quick extra. If the clerk forgets to ask, you can lead. Say, あたためてください. In English, please heat this. Say all three out loud once before your next trip.

<upbeat> Save this for your next trip to Japan.

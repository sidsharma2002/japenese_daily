## Instagram (reel.mp4, cover.png)

Someone offers you something, you say いいです, and they may hear no thank you.

いいです (ii desu) after an offer can read as good, or as I am fine without it.

Say yes clearly:
はい、お願いします (hai, onegai shimasu) = yes, please.
いただきます (itadakimasu) = I will gladly have it.

One exception: if someone asks いいですか (is this okay?), いいです really is yes.

Save this, you will want it later.

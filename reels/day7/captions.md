A polite maybe that is often a quiet no, and how to tell the difference.

検討します (けんとうします) = we will consider it. Often a gentle way to decline, though sometimes it is real thinking.

A real maybe comes with a date or a question: 検討して、金曜日までにお返事します。 = I will think it over and reply by Friday.

Nothing more after it? Ask: いつごろお返事をいただけますか。 = When can I hear back?
